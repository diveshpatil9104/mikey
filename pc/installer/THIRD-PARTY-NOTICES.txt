Owlmic includes the following third-party software.

Owlmic microphone
  The Owlmic microphone runs on VB-CABLE, the virtual audio cable driver by Vincent Burel (VB-Audio Software).
  Origin: https://www.vb-cable.com
  VB-CABLE is donationware; all participations are welcome.
  License: https://vb-audio.com/Services/licensing.htm

Owlmic Cam
  Owlmic Cam runs on softcam by tshino, under the MIT License.
  https://github.com/tshino/softcam
